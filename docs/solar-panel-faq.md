# Sunbeam Home Solar FAQ

> Synthetic sample document for demonstrating local-llm-rag. "Sunbeam" is a fictional company.

## How long is the panel warranty?

Sunbeam panels carry a 25-year performance warranty. Output is guaranteed to stay above 85% of the original rating.

## What maintenance is required?

Rinse the panels with water twice a year. Do not use abrasive cleaners. The inverter should be inspected every five years.

## Can I add a battery later?

Yes. All Sunbeam inverters are battery-ready. A battery can be added without replacing the inverter.
