## Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time. Outside core hours, staff choose their own schedule.

## Expenses

Expenses under 50 EUR need no approval. Larger purchases require sign-off from a team lead before ordering.

## Remote work

Staff may work remotely up to three days per week. Each team keeps one fixed in-office day.
